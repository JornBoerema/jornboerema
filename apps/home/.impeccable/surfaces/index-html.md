# Home (/) surface brief

Scope: the home page of the Even site, plus the look /privacy, /terms and /download inherit. Mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: Dutch entrepreneurs on Outlook, arriving from LinkedIn or a referral. They must understand in seconds that Even mailen is a fast mail app for Outlook, see it work, see the price, and start the 14-day trial. Proof on hand: the app's real shortcuts and features; no customers or numbers. Any inbox shown is made-up mail, labelled as an example.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is an opened ring binder, the entrepreneur's own administration. Each section is a divider sheet in one flat colour with its tab on the edge; mail is the sheet being filed. It refuses the dark centred hero with a glowing screenshot and a row of feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Flat saturated divider card: orange (Even), blue, yellow, green, black. Paper-white punched sheets with black rules. Two punched holes on the left of every divider. Tabs with rounded outer corners; binder spines with labels. One heavy grotesque for lettering, the same family lighter for reading. No gradients, no glass, no cards with icons.

STORY: This handles my mail fast, it is Dutch and private, the price is plain, the rest comes free. Then: start the trial.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Orange divider edge to edge. Brand top left. Headline "Even je mail doen." large at left, one line of explanation, black trial button under it. Right: a white punched inbox sheet, slightly rotated, six made-up mails, the top one marked yellow, with its keys beneath. Tab column fixed at the right edge: Mail, Erbij, Prijs, Vragen, Begin; the active tab sticks out further.

Signature interaction: the visitor files the example inbox with the app's real keys (E, B, T, J/K) or by clicking; each mail slides off under the tabs, the rest drop into place, the count runs to zero.

Raises: gravity sets the order (sheets drop one by one in one run); the tab column is identical on every section and is the page's ruler; one claim per divider; everything scales in whole units, a tab stays a tab on every screen.
