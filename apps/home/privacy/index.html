Privacy policy

This policy covers Even mailen, an email app that shows the Gmail and Outlook mailboxes you connect to it. Last updated 8 November 2026.

Who runs it

Even mailen is made by Jorn Boerema in the Netherlands, who is responsible for your data under the GDPR. Questions and requests can be sent through GitHub.

What the app accesses

When you connect a mailbox, you give the app permission through Google or Microsoft to read, label, move and send email in that mailbox. It also receives your name and email address, to show which mailbox is connected. The app only acts on your mail when you use it.

What is stored

  • Your Evido account: name, email address and sign-in details.
  • For each connected mailbox: its address, your name and encrypted access tokens.
  • Message details needed for your inbox list: sender, recipients, subject, a short preview, date, folder and read status.

Message bodies and attachments are not stored. They are fetched from Google or Microsoft when you open a message.

How your data is used

Your data is only used to show you your own mail and carry out what you ask the app to do. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models. Nobody reads your mail, unless you ask for help with a problem or the law requires it.

Even mailen's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Who processes it

The app runs on Vercel and stores data in a Neon database, both hosting data in the European Union where available. Google and Microsoft provide your mailbox. Ably may deliver live update signals, and Resend sends account emails. Your data is not shared with anyone else.

Security

Access tokens are encrypted at rest and never sent to your browser. All connections use HTTPS.

Deleting your data

Disconnecting a mailbox removes its tokens and stored details from the app straight away. Deleting your account removes everything linked to it. You can also withdraw access at any time in your Google account or Microsoft account.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, and you can complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.
